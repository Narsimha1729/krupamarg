# Krupamarg · कृपामार्ग

*The path of grace.* Register your sankalp, choose sacred sites, and follow a devotional plan of puja, vrat, jap and daan until your deadline — with live panchang, planet positions and a mantra counter.

Works in any browser and installs like an app on Android, iPhone and desktop.

- **Your data stays on your device** (browser storage). Download or delete it anytime from Profile.
- Panchang, grahas and dosha indications are calculated in the browser (low-precision model, Lahiri ayanamsa, about a degree of accuracy).
- Temple sankalp readings, poojas and seva bookings are **not live yet**; they will open with temple trusts' agreement, paid directly to each trust.

Prayer and guidance, not guaranteed results.
